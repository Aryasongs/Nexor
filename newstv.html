No statement yet from Gursharn Arya, his family, or Nexor on threat report

Nearly a day after reports of a threatening message allegedly sent from Pakistan, the Nexor Technologies founder, his family, and the company have all remained silent

Staff Report Updated 2 hrs ago
Editor's note: The claims below describe an absence of public comment, not confirmation or denial of the underlying threat. Nothing in this report should be read as verifying the origin of the earlier message.

No comment received

Repeated attempts to reach Gursharn Arya, his family members, and Nexor Technologies' official channels for comment have gone unanswered as of publication.

More than a day after news first broke of a threatening message allegedly directed at Nexor Technologies founder Gursharn Arya, an unmistakable silence has settled over the story. Neither Arya himself, nor any member of his family, nor a single official representative of Nexor has issued so much as a one-line statement addressing the matter.

That silence is, in itself, becoming part of the story. In cases involving alleged cross-border threats, a founder's public response, or the calculated decision to withhold one, is often read closely by observers, employees, and users of the affected company's products alike. So far, Nexor's usual communication channels, its website, its social handles, and any founder-linked accounts, show no acknowledgement that the report even exists.

"As of this report, not one word has come from Arya, his family, or the company he founded."

Sources close to the matter have not confirmed whether the silence reflects an ongoing security review, legal caution, a decision to let authorities handle the matter privately, or simply that the family has chosen not to engage with press inquiries during an active safety concern. All of these remain speculation until Nexor or Arya himself addresses it directly.

For a founder-led company like Nexor, whose products carry the Arya name and reputation closely tied to the brand, the absence of a statement carries its own weight. Employees and product users have reportedly begun asking questions on community channels, though none of these queries have received an official reply either.

This publication reached out through multiple available channels, email, listed contact numbers, and public social profiles associated with both Arya and Nexor. None of these attempts had produced a response by the time of publication.
