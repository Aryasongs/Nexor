04 — PATH TO MARS

OUR ROADMAP TO MARS BY 2040

OrionX's long-range vision is to design, build, and fly its own vehicle toward Mars within this generation. Everything below — the timeline, the vehicle, the numbers — is a concept roadmap and design target, not a funded, scheduled, or committed program.

PROGRAM TIMELINE (CONCEPT)

Target dates for planning purposes only — subject to change as engineering, funding, and testing progress.

2026 – 2028
Propulsion & Autonomy Testbeds
Ground and suborbital testing of a reusable engine line and the autonomous navigation stack that will fly on every later mission.
2029
ORX-01 · Lunar Orbit Demo
First flight of the technology-demonstration orbiter, validating deep-space communications and autonomy in cislunar space.
2031
Orbital Refueling Trial
In-orbit propellant transfer test — a prerequisite for sending a fully-fueled transfer stage toward Mars.
2033
ORX-02 · Mars Pathfinder
Uncrewed rover concept targets the Martian surface to scout candidate landing sites and prove out entry, descent, and landing systems.
2036
Cargo Pre-Deployment
Uncrewed cargo landers pre-position power, supplies, and habitat elements ahead of any future human-rated mission.
2038
Full-Stack Systems Test
PATHFINDER-M's transfer stage and habitat module validated together in Earth orbit ahead of departure.
2040
Target Mars Departure Window
OrionX's target window for its first attempt to send its own vehicle to Mars — the culmination of this roadmap.

05 — TECHNOLOGY

SYSTEMS WE'RE BUILDING

Autonomous Navigation

Onboard systems that plan and correct trajectories without waiting on ground control, cutting decision latency across millions of kilometers.

Orbital Intelligence

Software that fuses telemetry, imagery, and tracking data into a live operational picture of every asset in flight.

Deep Space Communications

Low-power, high-resilience links engineered to hold a signal across the growing distance of a deep-space trajectory.

Planetary Imaging

Multispectral sensor packages built to map terrain, composition, and atmosphere from orbit or descent.

Space Data Systems

Radiation-tolerant onboard computing and storage that keeps mission data intact in one of the harshest environments there is.

AI Mission Control

Ground-side models that flag anomalies early and help small teams operate complex missions safely.
