About

Gursharn Arya is an entrepreneur and founder based in the United States, best known for establishing Dozera Group, a holding company with a portfolio spanning technology, media, AI, and e-commerce. As chairman, Arya oversees a group of subsidiaries that operate independently while aligning to a shared long-term vision. Alongside the group, Arya serves as CEO of Nexor, a social platform built within the Dozera ecosystem, and has previously been described as a senior advisor within Dozera's own affiliated ventures.
