Kashish AI smart glasses
Built for the future. Powered end-to-end by Kashish AI.
IN DEVELOPMENT
Kashish IRIS is Nexor's upcoming hardware — a real pair of AI smart glasses. Hardware isn't shipping yet; here's exactly where things stand.
What IRIS is designed to do
Capture life, hands-free
A built-in camera in the frame for photos and video, no phone needed.
Kashish AI on-the-go
The same assistant you're chatting with now, running hands-free through the glasses.
Calls & notifications
Stay connected — calls, messages and alerts, without pulling your phone out.
All-day power
Battery built to last a full day of normal use on a single charge.
What's not available yet
No physical glasses yet
IRIS hardware hasn't shipped — there's nothing to buy or pre-order right now.
No in-app camera scanning
This app can't see through your phone's camera on IRIS's behalf — that only works once the actual glasses exist.
No release date or price yet
Both will be announced here, in the app, once they're locked in.
The design
Powered by Kashish AI
Every IRIS feature runs on the exact same Kashish AI you're already talking to in this app — nothing separate to learn, no second assistant. What IRIS adds is the hardware: a camera and speaker you wear, instead of a screen you hold.
Want to know when IRIS ships? Just ask Kashish in chat — she's kept up to date on IRIS and can fill you in any time.
